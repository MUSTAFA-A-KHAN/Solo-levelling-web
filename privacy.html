PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: August 22, 2026

1. DATA WE COLLECT

SYS:LEVEL collects only the data necessary to power your gamified progression: step counts, sleep data, activity metrics, and health data you choose to sync from your Android device via Google Fit.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

Your data is used solely to compute your in-app stats, XP, levels, and quests. We never sell your data. All processing occurs on your device whenever possible.

3. DATA STORAGE & SECURITY

Data synced from Google Fit is accessed only through the permissions you grant. We do not store raw health data on our servers beyond what is needed to maintain your progress.

4. YOUR RIGHTS

You may disconnect your health data source at any time from within the app settings. Doing so halts further data collection but retains your locally-stored progress.

5. CONTACT

Questions? Contact us at support@syslevel.app.

The System does not trespass on your privacy — only on your excuses.
