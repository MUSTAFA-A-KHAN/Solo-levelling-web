Questions

Good
to know.

Is SYS:LEVEL free?
Yes. Download the APK, install it on your Android phone and start levelling. There is nothing to buy to unlock quests, stats or achievements.
Which phones does it run on?
Android. The app reads from your phone’s health platform, so if your steps and sleep are tracked there, the System can see them.
What data does the System read?
Step counts, sleep, activity minutes and health metrics such as heart rate. Only what you grant access to, and you can revoke it any time from your phone’s settings. See the privacy policy.
Do I have to log anything by hand?
No. Once you connect your health data, everything syncs on its own and converts into XP, quests and attributes. You just live your day.
Do I need an account?
No. Install the app and grant access to your health data. One tap, no accounts to manage.
I found a bug. Where do I report it?
Send a transmission through the contact page, or open an issue on GitHub.
